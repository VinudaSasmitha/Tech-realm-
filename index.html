08The Codex · FAQ

Know the rules.

How does Learn & Earn work?

Each course has a fixed base value. As more properties are built in a territory, demand can raise it. The base funds the platform. The additional revenue is split in the illustrative model: Platinum 35%, Diamond 25%, Gold 16%, Silver 8%, King bonus 6%, and 10% for transaction and operational fees. It is not guaranteed income.

Will the AI give me the answers?

No. When you’re stuck, the assistant gives a short contextual hint. Copy-paste is restricted in assessments, and screenshot deterrence is used where the browser supports it. No website can fully stop screenshots or a phone camera.

What’s the difference between a home and a property?

Your home is built in your primary pathway’s territory across four tiers. After mastering it, each new role in another territory becomes a property: a wooden hut at Silver, a tribal hut at Gold, a traditional house at Diamond and a grand mansion at Platinum.

Are the Pathfinder’s results a guarantee?

No. Recommendations are suggestions to help you explore roles that match your interests. They are not guaranteed career outcomes.

How do team projects work?

From the Diamond tier, you build real-world projects in teams of around 3–5. Each completed project becomes a location in the realm, scaled to its complexity.
